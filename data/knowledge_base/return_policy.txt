Business Name: RoSin Bangles / RoSin Jewellery

Topic: Return, Refund & Exchange Policy

Strict No Return / No Refund / No Exchange Policy:
- All jewellery items are custom made to order. RoSin does not provide exchange, return, or refund under normal circumstances.
- No size exchange: Customers must check the size guide before ordering. Size exchanges or returns are strictly not accepted.

Transit Damage & Missing Items:
- Customers must notify RoSin via WhatsApp (+91 8438990370) within 2 days (maximum 3 days) of receiving the parcel.
- An uncut, continuous 360-degree unboxing video displaying all sides of the parcel and the shipping label before opening is mandatory. Without an unboxing video, no claim will be processed.
- Glass bangle breakage: 1 or 2 broken glass bangles is considered normal transit wear and is beyond control (no claim). If more than 6 glass bangles are broken, contact RoSin immediately with the unboxing video for review.
- Handmade variations: Slight handcrafted variations or minor stone loosening from transit are natural for handmade items and not considered manufacturing defects.

Product Care Instructions:
- Delicate bangles and handmade items are not designed for daily rough wear.
- Keep bangles away from soap and water. Remove before bathing or hand washing to avoid color fading or damage.
