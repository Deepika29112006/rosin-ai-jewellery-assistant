Business Name: RoSin Bangles / RoSin Jewellery

Topic: Shipping & Delivery Policy

Production & Handcrafting Times:
- Handmade items: All handmade items are made to order. It takes 7 to 10 business days to handcraft the jewellery before dispatch.
- Glass bangles: Dispatch takes 5 to 20 business days depending on ready stock availability. If ready stock is available, dispatch is immediate; otherwise, it takes up to 20 days as a pre-order.

Domestic Delivery (India):
- Domestic delivery within India takes 3 to 10 business days from the date of dispatch.
- Shipping charges are calculated based on parcel weight and delivery location, specified at checkout.
- Urgent orders / express dispatch: Available upon request by contacting WhatsApp at +91 8438990370.

International Delivery:
- RoSin ships internationally worldwide. International delivery typically takes 1 to 2 weeks.
- International orders must be placed directly by contacting WhatsApp (+91 8438990370) or Instagram DM (@rosin_bangles).
- Customs fees, import duties, and local taxes are the sole responsibility of the customer.

Order Tracking:
- Tracking details are sent via email and text message once dispatched.
- RoSin is not responsible for courier delays. If a parcel is not delivered within 15 days of dispatch, contact rosinfashionjewellery@gmail.com.
