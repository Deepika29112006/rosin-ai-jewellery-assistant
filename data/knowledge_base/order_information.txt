How to Order Custom Outfits-Matching Bangles:
- All bangle sets can be customized to match sarees, lehengas, or bridal outfits.
- Customers should send their outfit photo and preferred design/model via WhatsApp (+91 8438990370) or Instagram DM (@rosin_bangles).
- After design confirmation and advance payment, the set is custom handcrafted and dispatched.

Bangle Size Guide:
- Size 2.4: Inner diameter 6.0 cm
- Size 2.6: Inner diameter 6.3 cm to 6.5 cm
- Size 2.8: Inner diameter 6.7 cm
- Kids sizes: Available in the dedicated kids category with custom smaller diameters.
- Customers must measure carefully as size exchanges are not accepted.

Jewellery Rental Services:
- RoSin provides bridal imitation jewellery on rent for weddings and events.
- Rental inquiries are handled exclusively in person at the Pallipalayam physical store or via WhatsApp (+91 8438990370).
