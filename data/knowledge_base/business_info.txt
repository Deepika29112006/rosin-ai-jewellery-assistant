Business Name: RoSin Bangles / RoSin Jewellery

Topic: Business Identity & Artisan Heritage

RoSin is an Indian handmade jewellery and accessories brand founded in 2021 by Sindhu Senthilkumar. The business operates as a woman-led craft studio with skilled women artisans creating traditional and contemporary bangles, imitation jewellery, and hair accessories.
