🏛️ Our Story & Artisan Mission

RoSin was founded by Sindhu Senthilkumar in 2021. What began as a personal passion for creating intricate jewellery blossomed into a thriving small business dedicated to delivering unique designs and unmatched customer experiences.

When you choose RoSin, you are supporting a small business run by talented women artisans who pour their hearts into each piece. From traditional silk-thread bridal bangles to sparkling glass collections and chic daily wear, our accessories are designed to bring timeless joy.

🛡️ Return, Refund & Damage Policy

  • Strict No Return / No Refund / No Exchange: All items are custom made on order. RoSin does not accept returns, refunds, or exchanges.
  • No Size Exchanges: Size exchanges are strictly not accepted. Customers are advised to measure using our size chart before ordering.
  • Transit Damages & Mandatory Unboxing Video: In case of damage during transit or missing items, you must notify RoSin via WhatsApp (+91 8438990370) within 2 days of delivery. A continuous, uncut 360-degree unboxing video displaying all sides of the parcel and the sealed shipping label before opening is mandatory. Without this video, no action will be taken.
  • Glass Bangle Breakage: 1 or 2 broken glass bangles in transit is considered normal transit wear and beyond control. Claims are evaluated only if more than 6 glass bangles are broken, supported by the unboxing video.
  • Product Care: Delicate bangles are not waterproof and not for daily rough wear. Avoid contact with soap and water; remove while bathing or washing.
